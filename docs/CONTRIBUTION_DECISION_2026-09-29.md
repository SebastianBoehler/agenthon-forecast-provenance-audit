## Contribution gate

Before spending more time on a new study, write one sentence containing the demonstrated addition to knowledge. Require:

1. **Useful question:** a concrete researcher/user benefits from the answer, with a plausible use beyond the competition score.
2. **Bounded novelty:** compare directly with the nearest papers' methods and findings; acknowledge inherited components. A new combination is insufficient unless its consequence matters.
3. **Evidence:** an appropriate baseline, falsifiable prediction, controlled comparison and result that supports the claimed addition. Code availability or a planned experiment is not a result.
4. **Deliverability:** accessible inputs, existing artifacts and enough time to collect/analyze the necessary evidence before submission.
5. **Reviewer clarity:** readers can distinguish the problem, contribution, evidence and limitations without deciphering the engineering history.

A contribution can be a mechanism, empirical discovery, or validated benchmark. It need not create a new field. Its size is judged by what it enables or establishes, not by how grand the title sounds. Scientific review reduces avoidable weaknesses; it cannot guarantee acceptance.
