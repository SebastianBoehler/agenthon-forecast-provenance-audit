## Scientific risks in priority order

1. **Contribution and significance.** Familiar label repair and rounding are
   already covered by closest work. Our source-traced wrong-quantity mechanism
   and fixed-answer grading consequence must be clear in the abstract/introduction.
   The two known FinanceReasoning repairs are corroboration, not our new discoveries.
2. **Measurement validity.** AI agreement is not expert truth. Broad unit matching
   misses semantic types. The prior reminder changes calculation representation.
   Distinguish source fault, defensible interpretation, precision, format and
   financial reasoning; retain uncertainty and all attempt denominators.
3. **External validity.** The current corpora are selected discovery cases with
   unknown pretraining exposure and TAT report dependence. No new small pilot can
   establish population prevalence, held-out transfer or a causal family advantage.
4. **Presentation.** Lead with one clear financial failure and why the verifier
   accepts it. Keep the diagnostic extensions subordinate; a crowded paper with
   extra model architectures would obscure the contribution.
