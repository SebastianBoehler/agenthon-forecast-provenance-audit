## Executive summary (read this first)

The strengthened base is ready for author-led voice co-drafting. The paper remains
a draft, with no external submission. Additional local grader evidence and an
independently authored financial-code comparison improve the evidence and narrow
overclaims. They do not certify main-track sufficiency or predict acceptance.

### What we test and why it matters

An LLM reads a financial question and returns a final numerical answer. We ask
whether the released target computes the requested quantity and obeys the output
instruction, then keep model answers fixed while comparing grading decisions.
That separates an answer's quality from a grader's treatment of that answer.

The central result is release-specific: verified synthetic supervision can retain
financing debt as a call-price target or ignore explicit integer rounding. Tested
price bounds and regeneration against the same template can miss the quantity
substitution. The 12,655-row selected-family census, passing families and 800 saved
answers quantify these findings without claiming model-training harm or global
financial-dataset prevalence.

### Evidence added in the final iteration

- **Grader decomposition:** 500 MATH500 references, 368 admitted scalar-rational
  references and 2,548 authored controls separate numeric equivalence from string
  normalization. The native rule credits 354 sign-flip controls and rejects 372
  equal-value controls. These counts are dependent authored diagnostics, not
  natural benchmark error rates.
- **Local observations:** 96 scheduled Gemma/Qwen requests all return. Financial
  released-label scoring denies 16 of 28 contract-valid replies; the small natural
  mathematical panel shows no unequal-value credit. Strict formats, output caps,
  repeated questions, architectures and quantization limit model comparisons.
- **Independent author-code comparison:** 300 prospectively frozen native
  FinChain-template instances across three functions all pass source-rounding
  compatibility. Eighteen call values differ under exact visible-input evaluation;
  source intermediate rounding explains them. Thirteen zero/zero call/debt
  mutation collisions are nondiagnostic, while all 287 scalar-changing mutations
  are rejected. This is current-code breadth, not historical corpus replication.
- **Citation grounding:** 34 keys, 41 citation commands and 175 exact saved-text
  excerpt bindings pass the current-revision integrity check. Semantic author
  review remains pending; two source texts are unavailable to the integration.

Portable replay uses `scripts/replay_grader_comparison.py` and
`scripts/replay_independent_finance.py`. The latter also regenerates original
question/solution strings from licensed source fragments. Original freezes,
failed interfaces, null reminder findings and inconclusive optimizer branches
remain preserved. No additional paid inference was used for this iteration.

### Critical review and writing priorities

The statistical, financial/NLP and organizer documents are hypothetical review
lenses, not feedback from Hennig, Lu or actual workshop organizers. The
[organizer addendum](FINAL_ORGANIZER_FINCHAIN_ADDENDUM_2026-09-30.md) recommends
drafting now: keep core financial failures and paired grading consequences central,
with FinChain as a passing comparison and MATH500 as grader decomposition.

For voice co-drafting, work from the author's notes through introduction, methods,
core results, grader/source comparisons and discussion. Review a source excerpt or
experiment ledger for each claim. Reduce experiment detail in the main text and
retain complete denominators/amendments in appendices. Avoid presenting a new
universal verifier, expert benchmark or trained-model improvement.

Historical independent financial-release replication remains open. Financial
adjudication is absent; ambiguous document references remain provisional. Simple
identity-backed quantity/rounding findings do not require us to invent expert
authority. Main-track ambitions need broader released-source validation and
practical utility; causal training or downstream effects require their own study
if claimed. Those are future gates, not completed outcomes of this draft.
