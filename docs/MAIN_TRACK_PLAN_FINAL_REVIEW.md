## Material corrections and next gates

1. **Define the statistical target before drawing population conclusions.**
   The current hash order ranks individual questions before suppressing repeated
   native groups. Larger groups therefore have more opportunities to appear early;
   this is not a uniform sample of reports or questions. No probability-sampling
   design or inclusion weights were declared. Report future audit findings first as
   counts on these exact selected sets. Do not extrapolate an unweighted defect
   rate to either complete benchmark, financial reports generally or supervised
   finance releases. A future population estimand requires a separate prospective
   sampling/weighting design; do not replace or reselect these frozen 96 cases.

   TAT-QA's 48 context IDs are not 48 independent reports. Report clustering and
   cross-source underlying-report overlap remain unknown. Two annotation pipelines
   also do not create 96 independent observations of a pipeline-level phenomenon.
   Prespecify evidence units, paired contrasts, uncertainty and primary comparisons
   for the larger experiment. Two optimizer seeds support a bounded pilot result,
   not a well-estimated distribution of training effects. Clarify Stage 2's
   “randomly selected passing cases” as review of the prospectively sampled cases,
   including passing/ambiguous outcomes; targeted diagnostic cases are a separate
   set and cannot enter an unbiased prevalence denominator.

2. **Keep annotation breadth separate from verified-training-release breadth.**
   FinQA and TAT-QA offer separately described human annotation pipelines and useful
   document tasks. Neither preparation nor replay establishes an executable RLVR
   reward release, independent financial truth or naturally coexisting defects.
   FinChain remains a close baseline and unadmitted executable-generation candidate.
   Before a plural verifier-training claim, admit additional genuine reward/generator
   pipelines with lineage and executed-behavior evidence, or narrow the contribution
   to annotation/evaluation and prompt adaptation. The present plan already states
   most of this boundary; preserve it in any abstract and benchmark name.

3. **Freeze information access and task contracts before consequence testing.**
   The current pilot corrects assigned targets and the whole-unit constraint together;
   reflection sees assigned targets. It cannot isolate scalar-reward misspecification
   or label replacement alone. The roadmap acknowledges this. Larger ablations must
   match numerical acceptance geometry, serialization, target visibility, format
   and budget rules when isolating one mechanism; report actual actor/reflection costs.

   A direct-formula baseline for document QA may require human-selected evidence or
   a supplied financial identity. State who supplies those inputs, whether each
   competing method receives them, and the annotation/checking cost. Otherwise the
   comparator has privileged information. Freeze document-specific answer kinds,
   scale/sign/percentage/rounding conventions, context budget and separate native
   versus independent scoring before model calls. The pilot's four-family parser
   and synthetic oracle cannot silently become a general document-finance oracle.

4. **Budget the main experiment after a single mechanism survives adjudication.**
   The complete Stage 4 ablation table is a menu, not a simultaneously feasible
   commitment on the Mac or the pilot's sub-dollar budget. Prioritize one natural
   mechanism, cheap competitive controls and a genuinely held-out task/pipeline;
   specify data/review effort, model access, memory, wall time and actual compute
   before scheduling weight training. Installed PufferLib 3.0.0 was confirmed in
   the current runtime, but no trainer was run here. Installation or MPS inference
   does not establish RL training feasibility. No new architecture is needed merely
   to make the plan sound like a main-track paper.
