## Executive summary (read this first)

For the September 30 Agenthon call, **financial answer-contract validity is the best conditional new-paper lead**, because this project now has two distinct observed prompt/answer mismatches in independent training-oriented finance datasets. The first is nonidentification caused by hidden precision in an RLVR dataset: all checked golds remain mathematically possible if displayed rates were rounded, but the visible question does not identify the exact target. The second is an explicit rounding instruction that disagrees with stored answers in a CFA/FRM reasoning dataset. Neither is yet a demonstrated *reward* error or a field-wide defect. The market-cycle/world-model idea has a more ambitious mechanism but its external MarS pilot did not establish one. A different idea, decision-sufficient evidence acquisition, remains conceptual with no pilot. None can be called likely to be accepted on the present evidence.

This review uses full primary papers and first-party artifact pages where available. A September 2026 determinacy preprint was only available through its official abstract and open-data README: its full PDF routes through a reader form. I therefore treat its precise relation to our work as incompletely checked.

## The determinacy collision must be cited, not ignored

The first-party abstract of [Arcifa and Carra, *Fair and cheap*](https://montanaresearch.org/publications/mrf-2026-04/) says eight quant-finance task designs failed the rule that graded answers be determined by what the agent sees. Its [open-data README](https://github.com/Montana-Research-Foundation/open-data/tree/main/experiments/MRF-2026-04-task-design-collapse) says seven designs were retired before model runs and one was probed with two rollouts. This is direct conceptual prior for observable-answer validity; we cannot claim to invent it. The available summary concerns benchmark task *determinacy*, whereas our proposed test concerns two concrete training-dataset label mechanisms (hidden precision and requested-rounding conflict), a specified **training reward comparator**, false reward decisions, and a validated repair. That distinction is a hypothesis to substantiate, not an established novelty certificate. The official site links a PDF through a reader form, and the linked Zenodo record returned a data ZIP rather than full manuscript in this pass; a full-text comparison remains open.

More broad novelty collisions: [FinancialAuditBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.32835) newly released 90 audit tasks across six synthetic engagements with 11-model evaluation and expert-reviewed rubrics. A new generic finance benchmark, synthetic binder, or sparse rubric audit must reckon with this work. [When Alpha Disappears](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23959) and [What survives honest evaluation?](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27734) already study leakage-safe and search-aware trading evaluation. [The Price of Thought](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30705) tests whether more LLM reasoning improves economic trading outcomes. These make a generic finance-agent harness, leakage toggle, or extra-compute study weak choices without a distinct measured result.

## Prespecified go/no-go for the best lead

1. Freeze source revisions and separate *rendered-exact* from *explicitly rounded* operand semantics. Recompute labels independently using Decimal or numerically stable formula implementations, without executing untrusted dataset code. The first Cosimo template audit meets this narrow check; independent sample adjudication remains.
2. Reconstruct the **actual advertised reward or grading policy**. If no comparator is released, report a sensitivity curve across clearly labeled reconstructed comparators; never present mismatches as observed false rewards.
3. On two independent generators, classify wrong label, underspecified prompt, legitimate rounding, valid alternative formula, and extraction error. Have a second reviewer adjudicate a stratified sample, including clean negative controls.
4. Submit a short paper only if the consequence survives a realistic comparator and a second source, or if a strong single-source finding is explicitly scoped and impactful. A repair needs measured false-rejection reduction without a rise in false acceptance on corrupted calculations.
5. Archive prompt, visible operands, source row, recomputation, comparator result, adjudication, repair, source commit and analysis history. The artifact can implement the user's agent-native reproducibility principle without claiming that provenance itself is a novel method.

**Recommendation to the paper team:** pursue the reward-validity independent-source check next. In parallel, inspect the final already accepted user-turn paper as a legitimate fallback for the non-archival call, which welcomes prior work, if coauthors agree. Do not write a guaranteed-acceptance narrative or a market-simulator defect paper from the existing pilots.
