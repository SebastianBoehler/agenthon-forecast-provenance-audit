standards as a target, while Agenthon's own workshop CFP governs submission.
Formatting and bibliography count do not establish main-track readiness. The
main scientific gaps are held-out usefulness, reference semantics and incremental
novelty. The checklist below is an internal audit, not a completed official paper
checklist or an assertion of compliance.

## What would raise the scientific bar

Freeze a single quantity-audit procedure before unused report/context groups.
Resolve genuinely disputed financial references through qualified adjudication;
retain unresolved items rather than force convenient labels. Compare final-number,
native normalization, simple tolerance, executable arithmetic and quantity-aware
checks under identical interfaces. Report false acceptance/rejection, availability,
coverage and cost, with group-level dependence visible. A useful residual improvement
after these baselines would strengthen significance and transfer.

FinQA and TAT-QA are recognizable published anchors, not evidence of novelty by
themselves. Keep original questions and labels beside explicitly authored contrasts.
Year, denominator, relative-change/percentage-point and per-share/total contrasts
can make the language-grounding mechanism understandable. Hold pair members in
the same report-group partition and independently validate each changed target.

## What remains before a formal main-track submission

Measure the rendered main-body page count; the voluntary target is nine, including
figures/tables. Complete the actual official checklist with truthful justifications
and provide the review-access artifact. A real main-track submission would require
anonymity and its own current venue deadline; this named Agenthon/preprint draft
is not presently a NeurIPS main-track submission. The 2026 main-track deadline
has passed. For Agenthon, the official CFP permits any format/length and a single
PDF; its submission route and author attendance are separate practical checks.
