## Executive summary (read this first)

The contribution holds as a focused empirical audit of selected released financial
supervision. Its novelty is the specific numerical evidence and measured grading
consequences, not the general ideas that labels can be wrong, graders matter, or
research should be reproducible. The three critical reviews support a conditional
workshop-poster case. They do not establish acceptance, faculty approval, a general
benchmark contribution, or main-track significance.

These are AI-assisted reviews. The organizer lens uses public academic background
and the call for papers. Hennig- and Lu-informed lenses use available course and
supervision guidance; neither professor has reviewed or endorsed this manuscript.
No private course quotation or transcript is included in this review package.

## What the contribution now says

> Our contribution is a release-specific numerical audit: systematic wrong-quantity
> and rounding failures; diagnosis in separately pinned public generator/verifier
> code; and grading disagreements on unchanged model answers under explicitly
> reconstructed label comparators, with passing families and source-authored controls.

The strongest observation is that all 1,000 audited binomial labels match financing
debt and pass the tested call-price bounds, while 975 fail independent call valuation
under both checked compounding conventions. This exposes a particular semantic
failure that elementary bounds miss. The inspected public regeneration mechanism
provides a consistent explanation; the exact original dataset-generation revision
and execution of its production verifier are not established.

This goes beyond an isolated incorrect example through the complete selected-family
census, seven passing families, independent valuation identities, actual rejected
completions, preference-pair implications and paired grading controls. Nevertheless,
many rows instantiate the same template: 12,655 rows and 10,476 distinct questions
are not that many independent mechanism replications or a prevalence estimate.

Closest financial work already addresses precision, ambiguous labels and answer
representation: [FinanceReasoning](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.766/),
[FinChain](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.662/) and
[FinVerBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29586v1).
[Measurement Risk, §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.27374v2) explicitly distinguishes
inference policy from scoring policy on emitted answers. The fixed-answer design
is appropriate evidence here, but not a newly invented experimental principle.
The full collision assessment is in the [novelty review](POSTER_NOVELTY_REVIEW_2026-09-30.md).

## Concrete manuscript improvements completed

1. **Put provenance limits up front.** The abstract, opening example, figure
   caption, pinned-source paragraph and contribution boundary distinguish the
   dataset revision from separately pinned public code. The original production
   generation revision is explicitly unproven.
2. **Make the prior-work difference visible.** A new comparison table positions
   the release-specific evidence against label repair, observability and metric
   sensitivity. It claims neither firstness nor a new general verification method.
3. **Expose which defects cause the primary grading result.** Of 136 numerically
   valid strict-format DeepSeek answers, the reconstructed cent comparator denies
   55: 48 binomial quantity cases and seven Gordon rounding cases. The two passing
   model-panel families contribute zero. Admitting either checked compounding
   convention gives 56/137 in the disclosed post hoc analysis; it does not remove
   the main observation.
4. **Keep model comparisons honest.** Strict parsing succeeds for 0, 34, 0 and
   137 of 200 responses across the four models. Numerical extraction is separately
   post hoc. The two-model Gordon ordering reversal is family-specific; checkpoint
   comparisons do not identify a causal model-size effect.
5. **Keep one scientific story dominant.** The main body leads with numerical
   supervision and grading. The GEPA pilot's unchanged seed selection and failed
   consequence/repair gates remain disclosed; full details and the retained
   research-direction diagram now sit in appendices. No negative result was removed.

Cheap successful alternatives remain visible: an integer constraint plus half-unit
tolerance repairs the tested Gordon family; 5% widening succeeds on the tested DCF
controls. Neither result supplies a general method, and tolerance alone cannot
repair the binomial wrong quantity. No new inference or scientific rescoring was
needed for these revisions. The reporting renderer derives the new breakdown from
the saved ledgers; numerical algorithms, responses and frozen records remain unchanged.

## What still prevents a stronger assurance

There is no calibrated acceptance probability, actual organizer feedback, published
detailed scoring rubric or verified representative accepted/rejected sample. Review
judgment and program constraints remain outside this study. A relevant, carefully
bounded paper can still be rejected for limited breadth or significance.

The substantial remaining scientific gaps are independent finance-expert adjudication,
prospective evidence from independent generator pipelines, and successful controlled
adaptation or training if learning consequences become a central claim. The prepared
96 FinQA/TAT-QA packets are unreviewed admission material, not validation. A larger
set of rows from the same template or a rushed new architecture would not fill those
gaps. The [main-track roadmap](MAIN_TRACK_RESEARCH_ROADMAP_2026-09-29.md) states
the separate evidence gates.

For the current poster, the presenter should be able to explain the debt-versus-call
example, strict parsing coverage, conventional-rate sensitivity, cheap successful
controls, reconstructed-reward boundary and inconclusive adaptation outcome. These
are the points on which a skeptical reader can assess the result directly.
