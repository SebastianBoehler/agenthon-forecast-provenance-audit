### What the main track actually requires

The main call explicitly welcomes in-depth analysis yielding new insights into existing methods' limitations or behavior. The reviewer criteria assess technical support and completeness, reproducible clarity, community significance, and originality; new empirical insights can satisfy originality without a new method. Contribution types include General, Use-Inspired, Concept & Feasibility, and Negative Results, with different interpretations of the same criteria. [Main call](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers), [reviewer guidelines](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/ReviewerGuidelines).

My recommendation is **General empirical research** if the central contribution becomes a new, well-supported account of real supervision-pipeline behavior. Use-Inspired is appropriate only if the study is tied to a genuine external workflow and its needs. Negative Results is not an easier label: official guidance requires deeper analysis and an important, surprising change in understanding, beyond an unsuccessful experiment. [Reviewer guidelines](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/ReviewerGuidelines).

An inferred Hennig/Lu standard translates to four questions: what could falsify the claim; what is surprising beyond prior work; why these measurements and comparisons fit the question; and which assumptions/results the author can defend independently of AI assistance. Technical documentation is necessary, but it cannot establish significance on its own.

### A main-track hypothesis worth testing

**Candidate hypothesis, not an established result:** in independently authored released supervision pipelines, repairs aimed only at visible precision or output formatting can leave or mask wrong-quantity errors, producing consequential objective divergence; source-grounded diagnosis explains those outcomes and a frozen correction procedure retains its benefit on unseen releases and task families.

The possible main-track contribution is the **empirical mechanism and its transferable explanatory value**, supported by actual release provenance and counterfactual tests. It is not the proposition that bad labels exist, that reward errors matter, or that a checker should use the right formula. Even the candidate is not guaranteed novel: it needs an observed pattern whose controls and external predictions add something beyond the collision table.

Two distinctions must survive throughout:

- **Coexistence versus policy interaction.** Multiple defects in one source record require source evidence for each. A single defect behaving differently under tolerances is a defect–policy interaction, not three naturally interacting label defects. Both can be studied, but neither can be relabeled to sound stronger.
- **Ambiguity versus corruption.** A hidden-operand target compatible with rounded inputs is unidentified, not necessarily wrong. Adding exact operands changes information/specification. Evaluate that intervention separately from repairing a demonstrably wrong quantity or violated explicit rounding instruction.

If natural coexistence is absent, abandon that hypothesis and assess whether a cross-release diagnosis/transfer result is independently strong enough. Do not manufacture multi-error examples and call them natural discovery.

### Evidence gates before a main-track claim

These are proposed scientific gates, **not official NeurIPS acceptance thresholds**. Freeze quantitative margins after defining the units and baseline variability, before inspecting the corresponding held-out outcomes.

| Gate | Evidence needed | What falsifies or limits the claim |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Independent source scope | A prospective eligibility rule; generator/maintainer lineage; pinned releases; all admitted passing/error families. Keep current sources as discovery. Reserve previously uninspected independent pipelines for testing. | Only clones, paraphrases, or sources selected after finding errors: restrict to a discovery census. |
| 2. Semantic truth and provenance | Independent problem interpretation before labels/diagnosis are shown; explicit financial conventions; documented adjudication of disagreements; independent identities; source-to-code mapping with uncertainty retained. Human domain review tests a different weakness from another arithmetic implementation. | Convention-dependent or missing-information cases dominate; do not convert them into definitive error labels. |
| 3. Natural interaction | Find actual records or pipeline stages with coexisting mechanisms. Compare relevant individual and joint corrections on the **same** cases/outputs, with units, tolerances and feedback matched. | Effects are additive, absent, or solely explained by acceptance geometry/format: drop the interaction claim. |
| 4. Consequence | Fixed-output grading plus a prospectively frozen adaptation intervention if claiming adaptation. Include seed/manual formula instructions, cheap rounding+tolerance controls, passing families, full failures, and both assigned/gold scores. | Search never improves its objective, or only formatting changes: adaptation is inconclusive; grading evidence remains separate. |
| 5. External transfer | Leave-release and leave-task-family evaluation of a frozen diagnosis/correction procedure or adapted policy. Separate new operands, new wording, new generator, and new financial identity. No test-based rule/prompt selection. | Benefit disappears on unseen releases/tasks, or requires case-specific answers from the evaluation oracle: narrow to known-family correction. |
| 6. New explanatory value | Predeclare what the stage diagnosis predicts beyond aggregate error rate and obvious tolerance/formula baselines. Verify those predictions externally, including negative cases. | Only “more corrupted labels give more disagreement”: no new explanatory discovery. |
| 7. Meaningful scope | Demonstrate why a reproducible synthetic-supervision or evaluation workflow would change its decisions; bound audit effort and remaining failures. Separate actual downstream use from hypothetical relevance. | No demonstrated operational or scientific decision changes beyond the already obvious repair of one generator. |

For source transfer, a second independent held-out pipeline is a useful robustness check against another single-generator anecdote; it is not a magic sample size or evidence of field-wide prevalence. Report the denominator of source lineages and task families, not only rows. Cluster uncertainty at the actual sampling unit when a defensible sampling model exists; finite census counts need no decorative row-level intervals.

“Frozen procedure” need not mean a universal automatic checker. If new families require manually supplied identities, specify who supplies them, what information they receive, and how much effort is allowed before opening test labels. That can evaluate a reproducible audit practice; it cannot establish automatic semantic verification.

### Bounded controls with the highest information value

First perform cheap source/semantic checks and score fixed outputs. A relevant partial-versus-joint repair design can separate input observability, quantity computation and requested projection; use only combinations supported by the actual cases, rather than forcing an eight-cell factorial where mechanisms are absent. Freeze tolerance centering and serialization. The passing WACC boundary cases already show why this matters.

The obvious alternatives must remain competitive: integer plus half-unit Gordon tolerance; independent formulas; call bounds; a correct manual finance prompt; and the DCF tolerance condition that currently succeeds. A sophisticated procedure matching these may still support a useful discovery, but cannot claim algorithmic superiority. Learned adaptation is justified only for an adaptation question; weight training is necessary only if the claim explicitly concerns weight-training effects.

The immediate GEPA pilot is a **feasibility gate**, not the main-track endpoint. A positive outcome motivates controlled replication across an independent actor/access path and genuine held-out sources/tasks. A null result or successful manual baseline must be kept; do not switch optimizers/architectures until an appealing effect appears. New recurrent depth, world models, or RL machinery require a separate mechanism-specific hypothesis and do not substitute for these gates.

### Main track versus E&D: make the choice from the final contribution

The user's target remains the **main track**. A main submission is defensible if the central result is a significant new explanation of supervision/optimization behavior with controlled and transferable evidence. If the strongest eventual contribution is instead an evaluation audit, maintained corrected corpus, or reproducible evaluation protocol, the official E&D scope explicitly welcomes those, with equally stringent review. That is a fit distinction, not a lower bar or an automatic replacement recommendation. [E&D call](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForEvaluationsDatasets).

A benchmark claim additionally needs a documented construct, legitimate accessible data, durable splits, clear intended use, leakage controls, and sufficiently independent tasks/labels. E&D requires appropriately hosted accessible code/data and dataset metadata. Both 2026 tracks had the same already-past deadlines; submissions cannot be made to both simultaneously or switched afterward. [E&D call](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForEvaluationsDatasets), [main call](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers).

Non-archival workshop work is permitted as prior work for a later main submission under the current handbook; a workshop result does not establish main-track readiness. Recheck the future cycle's policies when its call is available. [Main handbook](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook).

### Decision and claim boundary

Pursue the main-track target by earning **independent-source discovery, controlled mechanism isolation, and external prediction/transfer**, with the present audit as discovery evidence. A new algorithm is optional; a new, useful conclusion is essential. The proposed hard gates can reject the attractive interaction hypothesis and keep the work honest. The current audit and even a positive single-release two-seed prompt pilot do not yet justify “naturally interacting defects cause general learning harm” or “general repair transfers across financial supervision.”
