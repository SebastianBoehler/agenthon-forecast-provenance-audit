## Objections that matter

| Objection | Evidence and judgment |
|---|---|
| Synthetic rows are not independent scientific replications. | The 12,655 rows instantiate a small selected set of generator families. Distinct prompts still share formulas, operand grids and generation logic. Two unambiguous defective families in one release are the principal mechanism evidence; CAPM/DCF are conditional ambiguity findings. The existing lack of row-level confidence intervals is appropriate. |
| These are reconstructed graders, not an actual training reward. | True. Fixed model responses make grading disagreement observable, but no deployed scorer, adoption, preference optimization or training harm was reproduced. This defeats a deployment claim, not the paired comparator experiment. |
| The strict grammar overwhelms the model comparison. | Qwen3 1.7B and Coder each have 0/200 strict parses; 4B has 34/200. Only DeepSeek supplies broad strict-format coverage. Four configurations do not constitute four independently informative confirmations of the headline. Unparsed does not mean mathematically wrong. |
| The supplementary extractor was chosen after seeing outputs. | True and already disclosed. The primary DeepSeek effect exists before recovery. The family ordering reversal depends on post hoc extraction; missing units and unrecovered punctuation prevent a latent-ability or general model-ranking interpretation. |
| The reference assumes simple rather than continuous rates. | The prompt is implicit. DeepSeek's one declared mismatch is compatible with continuous compounding. Admitting either convention strengthens its denial count by one, while all 975 source conflicts survive. Do not call that response an unequivocal arithmetic error. |
| WACC disagreement does not imply a bad source label. | Two 4B responses fall in the exact-formula cent window but outside the cent-rounded-label window. Both source labels pass. This is comparator geometry and should be separated from defective supervision. |
| Repair and its judge share the answer definition. | Correct. Perfect acceptance of repaired targets is oracle consistency. Separate Fraction arithmetic and valuation identities help detect implementation errors; they do not supply independent semantic adjudication. Source-authored wrong answers and actual unchanged model outputs are the substantive additional controls. |
| The inspected commit may not have generated the release. | Correct. All 1,000 golds numerically match financing debt, and the inspected public generator/verifier embody the matching mechanism. Exact historical generation commit, upstream execution trace and verification history remain unproven. |
| Artifact history is not new science. | Traceable evidence and retained failures support credibility. A retrospective custom graph, good TikZ and an ARA-inspired package do not establish methodological priority, completeness of the search history, official certification or benefits of the format. |
| GEPA supplies no adaptation result. | All four selected strategies are the seed. Both consequence/repair gates fail for both seeds; 37 overlong proposal batches constrain the pilot. Same-prompt holdout variation cannot be attributed to optimization. It is an inconclusive feasibility experiment, not evidence of training harm or harmlessness. |

None of these objections falsifies the narrow binomial/integer-label findings.
Several would invalidate a stronger title, general-method claim, model-ability
ranking or deployed-impact interpretation. Most limits are acknowledged in the
current body; the remaining risk is what a poster reader infers from its headline.

## Five surgical changes using existing evidence

1. **State the empirical novelty in one sentence.** Use: “In selected released
   financial supervision families, we trace a wrong-quantity computation and
   omitted integer rounding, then measure how reconstructed label comparators
   grade the same saved model answers.” Place the source-pin/history qualification
   next to the pipeline diagram. Contrast this evidence with existing financial
   precision/label-validation work; do not sell the numerical-contract principle
   or artifact schema as new.
2. **Make the experimental units visible.** Put 594/630 distinct Gordon questions
   and 888/905 distinct binomial questions beside 946/1,000 and 975/1,000 rows.
   Say these are two selected defective generator families, not independent
   template replications or corpus-wide prevalence. Keep the seven passing families
   and conditional CAPM/DCF findings visible as controls and interpretation checks.
3. **Use the mechanism split above as the hero consequence panel.** Label it
   “reconstructed comparator; fixed responses,” give strict coverage, and show
   passing controls. Keep the two-model Gordon ordering reversal in a clearly
   separate exploratory panel. Describe the four-model roster as measurement
   coverage, not a capacity or parameter-count experiment.
4. **Put semantic and precision objections beside their evidence.** Show the
   single continuous-rate answer and the unchanged 975 source conflicts in a compact
   sensitivity note. Give the WACC window-center example as a passing-label control.
   Retain the simple integer-plus-half-unit baseline and the 34 rounded erroneous-
   trace collisions. Together they clarify what tolerances and final-answer checks
   can and cannot establish without another experiment or broader parser.
5. **Separate empirical findings from consistency/resource results.** Label the
   perfect repair table as finite reference consistency, with manually specified
   contracts and AI-assisted validation. Keep the verified artifact/replay as
   reproducibility support. Report GEPA's all-seed selection and failed gates in
   the retained-results record; do not interpret its 432 repeated calls as 432
   successful adaptation trials. Human financial review remains pending.

These changes make existing evidence harder to misread. They cannot manufacture
independent-source replication, deployed-evaluator evidence, conceptual blindness,
external expert review or causal training effects. Those gaps cannot be resolved
by more caveats, improved typography or multiplying template variants.

## Recommendation and evidence identity

I would support a focused audit-friendly workshop poster because the wrong
quantity is specifically diagnosed, the obvious rounding baseline is informative,
and the primary paired grading consequence is real within its declared comparator.

a new general verifier, historical proof of the exact released pipeline, or
positive learning/adaptation effects. This is not a strong main-conference
recommendation and cannot be made “110% accepted.”
