## What we are testing in NLP terms

An answer can fail at four different stages: identifying the requested financial
quantity, calculating it, expressing its value/unit/scale, or being graded against
the released label. We test those stages separately. A year, denominator, net/gross
qualifier or percentage convention changes the meaning even when every number is
copied correctly and the arithmetic executes. Native tokenization is an inference
prerequisite; token encoding is not this paper's proposed contribution.

The central empirical contribution remains the released debt-for-call supervision
mechanism, its passing numerical bounds, source-level diagnosis and measured
denials of compatible saved answers. Financial label repair, interpreter use and
LLM judging have prior art. The new studies test how far the evidence extends and
where its interpretation must be narrowed.
