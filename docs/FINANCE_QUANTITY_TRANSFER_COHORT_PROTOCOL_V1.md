pool. Verify acquisition/selection hashes before reuse. The local 32-question
pilot is entirely within the earlier 96-question cohort; the exclusion union
therefore contains 96 unique questions. Exclude every earlier exact context and
question/context hash, plus every earlier FinQA company prefix or TAT native
context/group identifier. Select at most one FinQA question per new company and
one TAT question per new native context. No outcome/difficulty filtering or
replacement follows review, parsing, ambiguity or model failure.

`finance-quantity-transfer-v1-20260930`, source, native ID and existing
question/context hash. Apply FinQA then TAT, retaining exact-context/question
exclusions across both sources. Shortfall is an error requiring a disclosed
amendment before further selection; do not change grouping silently.

receipt at this stage. No answer/program/derivation enters the reviewer packets.
FinQA's canonical original input remains dataset/test.json; the native evaluator
copy may differ and is not substituted. TAT inputs remain the original raw context

recover annotations without fuzzy repair.

question-only AI technical reviews record requested quantity, entity/year,

alternative interpretations. Preserve insufficient/ambiguous references and lock
all reviews before source-target unblinding or selected model requests. Agreement
establishes numerical/typed-unit consistency only; conceptual errors may correlate.

and provider/cost bounds must be frozen before selected model inference. Compare

simple tolerance and a fixed label-free quantity review. Keep calculation grammar
identical across prompt arms. A useful residual result on this cohort can support
bounded transfer; it cannot establish a general audit method or population prevalence.
