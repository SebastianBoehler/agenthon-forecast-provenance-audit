## Executive summary (read this first)

The pinned public MarS 2m checkpoint ran through an experimental direct CPU client and the official exchange. A prespecified 30-seed by two-direction bank produced 52 completed 100-unit cycles, 8 initialization failures, and just one negative-cost completed cycle. This finite result does not support a reliable exploitable-cycle or model-defect claim. The pilot remains an infrastructure and protocol artifact, not a paper result.

## Prespecified outcome table

| Direction | Attempts | Completed | Initialization failure | Negative cost | Zero cost | Positive cost | Median completed cost | Range |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Buy then sell | 30 | 26 | 4 | 1 | 2 | 23 | 10,000 | −10,000 to 40,000 |
| Sell then buy | 30 | 26 | 4 | 0 | 4 | 22 | 10,000 | 0 to 90,000 |
| Total | 60 | 52 | 8 | 1 | 6 | 45 | 10,000 | −10,000 to 90,000 |

Cost is negative change in simulator cash, in price × volume units, conditional on a closed actual-fill cycle with no open order. A negative cost means cash profit. The single negative-cost case is buy–sell seed 21: −10,000. There were 1,062 model calls over all 60 attempts. All 52 completed cycles ended at zero inventory and zero open orders. The eight failures occurred at initialization seeds 7102, 7107, 7127 and 7128, before the learned model ran, with official `LobSnapshot.mid_price` raising `ValueError('unknown mid price')` after a noise-initialized book became empty. Each affected both directions and remains in the denominator.

The per-episode chart is generated by `figures/mars_cycle_v3.py` as `figures/generated/mars-external-v3/cycle_costs.pdf` and a PNG preview. The two series are separate simulation runs under the same seed, not two simultaneous trades.

## What this does and does not show

The official source and exchange were exercised with original stochastic decoding and converters, but the serving transport was local CPU fp32 with Ray 2.45 imported instead of the repository's CUDA/fp16 HTTP/Ray server route. The initial book was generated by the official example's `NoiseAgent`, not a historical market reset. The exchange in this pilot has no fee, borrow cost or self-trade-prevention constraint. We used actual fills and explicitly closed inventory; market close itself does not liquidate.

The one profitable simulation can arise from ordinary book movement over the cycle and is not evidence of a learned response defect. No historical counterfactual market or causal treatment effect is available. A publishable claim would require a reference market or a robust response-calibration experiment, leakage-safe data, multi-model replication, and fidelity-preserving repair. The original synthetic MLP violation also disappeared with more optimization. **Current contribution gate: fail.** Do not submit a paper claiming a discovered MarS arbitrage or broadly defective learned market responses from these results.

## Reproduction

With the isolated `.venv` and pinned ignored MarS assets already staged:

```bash
USE_TF=0 USE_FLAX=0 PYTHONPATH=src:data/external-baselines/mars-2m-pinned-v1/source .venv/bin/python scripts/run_mars_cycle.py --bank
```

The run code is in `src/mars_cycle/`. The audit was a research run, not a package test suite. Git status cannot be read with the current Apple command-line-tool setup because `git` exits on an unaccepted Xcode license; no license was accepted and no commit was made.
