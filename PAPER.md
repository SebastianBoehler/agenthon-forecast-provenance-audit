## Executive summary (read this first)

The current manuscript is [When Verified Financial Labels Fail](paper/answer_contract_voice_draft.tex).
This index distinguishes the paper's numerical evidence from exploration history.
Exact replay establishes recorded decisions; it does not establish financial meaning,
expert agreement, general defect prevalence or causal training effects.
